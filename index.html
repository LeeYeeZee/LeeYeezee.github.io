About

I am currently a core engineer on Krypton, a next-generation real-time serving and analytics system within ByteDance's Data Infrastructure organization. My work focuses on building LLM evaluation and data preview infrastructure for the Seed Foundation team. Through the Merlin model evaluation platform, I bring multimodal data management, unified offline-online queries, and real-time analytics together in a unified evaluation data foundation.

I believe a scalable LLM evaluation system requires not only well-designed metrics and benchmarks, but also a strong data engineering foundation. My focus is on the latter: unifying training data, human annotations, model outputs, and evaluation results; connecting multimodal data spanning text, labels, embeddings, logits, images, video, and audio; and providing stable, low-latency queries that make evaluation data accessible, analyzable, and ready for continuous iteration.

To support this goal, I designed and built Krypton's lakehouse architecture from the ground up. Its External Catalog connects heterogeneous sources including Elasticsearch, Apache Iceberg, and Hive, enabling unified reads, writes, and queries across multimodal datasets while supporting a 99.98% production SLA for Merlin. I also designed a hybrid row-column storage format and adaptive read path, achieving a 230% performance improvement on TPC-DS and a 500% improvement on tables with more than 2,000 columns, providing the performance foundation for interactive exploration and complex analysis of large-scale evaluation data.

Previously, I was the technical lead for the Alibaba AliExpress Feature Platform, where I built an integrated platform covering feature development, management, and online serving. I consolidated seven feature-query applications into one and reduced the feature production and synchronization workload for data analysts by 50%. The platform also enabled shared data assets across business domains, reducing duplicated feature development.

At Ant Group, I was a founding member of both LedgerDB and GeaBase. LedgerDB combined blockchain-grade verifiability with the performance of centralized systems, achieving 300,000 TPS per ledger and more than 80× the attestation-write performance of Hyperledger Fabric. This work was published at VLDB 2020. GeaBase powered Alipay's risk control, social features, Ant Forest, and the Chinese New Year Five Fortunes campaign. During the campaign, its core path sustained more than 2 million writes and 1 million one-hop graph queries per second.

I hold a master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Cornell University and a bachelor's degree in Automation from Chongqing University. Outside my core work, I explore AI-assisted development, applying LLMs to data formats, code understanding, content generation, and music creation.
